Case Study

Automated approval and reporting steps for an SME operations team to improve turnaround speed.

A workflow automation project focused on removing duplicated admin effort and improving ownership across internal operational steps.

The problem

The operations team depended on manual handoffs, status chasing, and repeated data entry across approvals and internal reporting. That created delays and inconsistent ownership.

The solution

We mapped the internal workflow, introduced automated routing and status tracking, and added reporting views so managers could see task movement and pending actions without manual follow-up.

The outcome

  • Faster turnaround across internal process steps
  • Reduced duplicated admin effort
  • Stronger accountability through clearer ownership and status visibility

Stack

  • Workflow automation
  • Dashboard reporting
  • Flask-based business logic
